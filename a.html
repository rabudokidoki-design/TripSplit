Executive summary: background and objectives

In 22 weeks, [Company] moves from no stated baseline controls to a minimum set of controls with evidence, so management can see the remaining risks before the IPO application.

Background
The business
[Company] is a clinical-stage biotech with about 200 staff, based in Hong Kong with offices in Hong Kong and Mainland China.
The trigger
[Company] plans an IPO application within about 6 months. IPO advisers, once appointed, may ask how systems and data are protected.
The starting point
[Company] states it has no baseline cybersecurity controls; [We] verify this in Week 1. Data sits in finance, cloud, staff devices and paper notebooks.
The constraint
The runway is short. Controls need time to run and leave evidence, so the quick start fixes begin in Week 1.
Objectives
Know the gaps
Assess 14 control domains against recognised international references, using interviews and evidence checks.
Plan it
Rank gaps by threat and build a 22-week roadmap sized for about 200 people, with an owner for each fix and three decision meetings (gates).
Close the minimum gaps
Put a starting set of minimum controls in place (about 15). [Company] confirms the final list at the first gate, Week 6.
Evidence it
Keep dated records for every control, so management can see what is in place and what risk remains.
Target at Week 22 — The minimum controls are in place and evidenced, and management has accepted the residual risks. [We] do not claim IPO readiness or compliance.

NOT A CYBER REFERENCE
Appendix C2 (ESG Reporting Code) is not a cybersecurity reference.

Why it fits: one ERP serves both offices and handles vendor payments; approval steps to be confirmed in Week 1.

Why it fits: staff use their own PCs and laptops for work; the standard for them is to be confirmed in Week 1.

Why it fits: lab notes are on paper at two sites and datasets sit in a Mainland cloud; leaver and record practice to be confirmed.

After Week 22, with generalist IT and a part-time coordinator. If it fails, fold it into another control or defer it.
